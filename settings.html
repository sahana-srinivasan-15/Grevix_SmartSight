Challenge Card Context & Architecture

Core design rules developed for the GREVIX AFTERCODE Challenge Card 2.

The Safety Blindspot Problem

Traditional AI surveillance systems report "Zero Hazards Detected" whenever their computer vision pipeline returns no bounding boxes. But when a camera disconnects, freezes on a static frame, or delays image transmission, an empty detection list is not proof of safety — it is a dangerous blindspot.

The Three Core Architectural Rules

1
Feed Freshness ≠ AI Confidence ≠ Site Safety

Never combine these metrics into a single arbitrary score. A stale frame tells you nothing about active conditions.

2
Fail-Safe Default to UNKNOWN

Any stale, offline, duplicate, or corrupted image immediately sets current site risk to UNKNOWN and triggers an inspection mandate.

3
Strict Recovery Handshake

A camera cannot clear its alert until a fresh, verified frame is successfully ingested.

Zero-Trust Guarantee

SmartSight runs client-side in the browser. Zero video frames are transmitted to external cloud servers, guaranteeing complete physical privacy.
