The Safety Blindspot Problem

Traditional AI surveillance systems report "Zero Hazards Detected" whenever their computer vision pipeline returns no bounding boxes. But when a camera disconnects, freezes on a static frame, or delays image transmission by 15 minutes, an empty detection list is not proof of safety — it is a catastrophic blindspot.

The Core Architectural Rule

1
Feed Freshness ≠ AI Confidence ≠ Site Safety

Never combine these metrics into a single arbitrary score. A 99% confident AI detection on a 10-minute-old frame tells you nothing about a worker currently entering a trench.

2
Fail-Safe Default to UNKNOWN

Any stale, offline, duplicate, or corrupted image immediately sets current site risk to UNKNOWN and triggers an inspection mandate.

3
Strict Recovery Handshake

A camera cannot clear its alert until a cryptographically unique, fresh frame with ordered timestamps is successfully ingested and validated.
